About the organism

Physarum polycephalum is a single cell with no brain and no neurons, yet it finds the shortest path through a maze and — given oat flakes laid out as Japanese cities — grows a network close to the Tokyo rail map.

Each agent here follows Jeff Jones’ 2010 model: sample the chemical trail at three points ahead, turn toward the strongest, step forward, and deposit a little trail of your own. That single rule, run a million times a frame, is the whole colony. Nothing below is drawn — every vein you see is agents agreeing with each other.
